## BIO

I studied biomedical engineering and machine learning at Boston University. I am now a Technical Solutions Engineer at Epic.

## Work in the lab

In the summer of 2025 I was a Distinguished Summer Research Fellow in the lab, where I studied how neural activity shapes decisions and actions.

## Summer project

My project asked how the activity of hundreds to thousands of neurons, recorded at the same time, relates to the decisions and actions we make. A single neuron's spikes rarely map cleanly onto a movement, so we look for hidden patterns across the whole population. Principal component analysis (PCA) can find those patterns, but it cannot say what they mean. I worked with demixed principal component analysis (dPCA), which separates the patterns into interpretable parts: one that follows time, and others that depend on the condition, such as the direction of a movement. Methods like this could one day help brain-machine interfaces read out a person's intended movement, even when they cannot physically move.
