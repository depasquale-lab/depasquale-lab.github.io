## Research summary

My lab develops biologically grounded computational models, machine learning systems that respect the constraints and dynamics of real neural circuits, to understand how the brain gives rise to perception, decisions, and movement. We build statistical models that infer hidden states from neural and behavioral data, including the open-source package [StateSpaceDynamics.jl](https://github.com/depasquale-lab/StateSpaceDynamics.jl); biophysically constrained network models that ask how circuits compute; and interpretable AI models, such as [LORAX](https://doi.org/10.1101/2025.11.04.686628) for olfaction and [BiXformer](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.06.05.730511) for communication between brain regions, whose learned representations reveal biological mechanisms. We work closely with experimental collaborators at BU and beyond. The [Research page](../) has our projects and figures.
