## BIO

I am a mathematics student at Boston University. I am focused on AI safety, alignment, and mechanistic interpretability, and I also enjoy reading and writing math articles.

## Work in the lab

I joined the lab in 2024 as an undergraduate. I contributed to StateSpaceDynamics.jl, the lab's Julia package for probabilistic state space models, and am a co-author on its paper in the Journal of Open Source Software.
