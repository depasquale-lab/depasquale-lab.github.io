The [Economo Lab](https://economolab.org/), led by Michael Economo, seeks to identify and understand the neural circuits in the brain that control the body, with a focus on the circuits underlying movement. We collaborate on analyses of motor cortex activity during movement and on models of inter-regional neural dynamics. [Learn more about their research.](https://economolab.org/research/)
