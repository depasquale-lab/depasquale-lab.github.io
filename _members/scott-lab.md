The [Scott Lab](https://www.scottcognitionlab.com/), led by Ben Scott, aims to identify the mechanisms of cognition by developing and applying new neural technologies. They study how animals integrate information over time to adapt their behavior to uncertain and changing environments, how these circuits are altered in conditions such as autism, and how learning shapes population-level neural activity. We collaborate on models of decision-making in rodents. [Learn more about their research.](https://www.scottcognitionlab.com/research-1)
