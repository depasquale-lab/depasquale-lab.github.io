BIO

I am an undergraduate at Boston University studying math and data science. I focus on probabilistic modeling, machine learning, and statistical programming, and I build inference systems for complex time-series data in neuroscience and finance.

Work in the lab

I joined the lab in 2024 and completed UROPs in the spring and fall of 2025. I am a co-author on the StateSpaceDynamics.jl paper in the Journal of Open Source Software and on the preprint on diurnal rhythms of decision-making in rats.
